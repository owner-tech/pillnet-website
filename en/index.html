Features

More than buying and selling

PillNet is a full working platform for the sector, not just a price list.

Organised orders

The pharmacy builds and sends its order, the warehouse accepts and prepares it, and both follow the status as it changes.

Discount and bonus comparison

Medicine prices are fixed nationally in Jordan, so warehouses compete on discounts and bonuses. PillNet shows them clearly.

One wallet

Balance, commissions and rewards in a single wallet, with a full record of every movement.

Sales missions

A warehouse posts a mission with a reward, a pharmacist agent carries it out, and the reward is paid once the order completes.

Sector jobs

Warehouses and pharmacies post vacancies, and pharmacists apply with their CV from inside the app.

Notifications and offers

Instant alerts for orders, offers and announcements, with full control over what you receive.
